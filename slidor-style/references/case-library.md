# Case library — what to steal from each

Source: the 25 case pages on slidor.agency/productions (395 slide images reviewed one by one, Oct 2026),
plus Lucile Mennessier's luxury portfolio (see `lucile-mennessier.md`). Slidor lists its featured work
first; **Tier 1 = the 11 featured 2023–2025 cases — copy these moves first.** Tier 2 = older work
(2017–2021): still useful for structure, but its decoration (confetti shapes, heavy 3D characters,
stock-photo people) looks dated — don't copy the ornament.

Never reuse their images, logos or copy — study the system, not the assets.

---

## Tier 1 — featured, 2023–2025

### Nespresso — strategic corporate deck (2024) → preset **atelier**
- Cream `#EFE8DF` base, caramel accent, espresso ink; warm product-in-kitchen photography.
- **Giant white chapter numerals (1, 3, 4, 5) set *behind* the product** — the cup/machine overlaps the digit (depth). Outline variant of the numeral on light slides.
- Titles: light caps line + bold caps line ("INCOME / THE SCENE"); pill button "Away 3" + circle arrow.
- Agenda with numbered rows and times + half-bleed photo. 4-up portrait photo cards with icon chips; one solid caramel text card among photos.
- Persistent footer bar: logo mark, confidential note, page number.
- *Steal*: numeral-behind-product, light/bold title, the warm restraint.

### LVMH × Dior — luxury trends deck (2023) → **editorial**
- Full-bleed, often AI-generated editorial images framed **inside a white inset with rounded corners**.
- **Huge Didone numerals cropped by the canvas** (")8.1", "01") over B&W portraits.
- Extended grotesk caps, white + one accent word (yellow `#FFC21A` or violet) — "EXPECTATION / WITH RAIN".
- Stats (66 % / 37 % / 17 %) with flag chips set directly on landscape photos; two tall photo cards each with a big % bottom-left.
- Header bar: section label left · "LVMH · DIOR" co-brand center · page right.
- *Steal*: inset rounded frame, cropped Didone numeral, accent-word title, stats-on-photo.

### Cartier — marketing deck (2025) → **maison**
- Cartier red textured paper, ivory paper, aubergine; **gold-foil wide-tracked caps** ("PART ONE / INTRODUCTION").
- Product cut-outs (watches, necklaces) floating on paper with soft shadows; heritage collage of vintage photos.
- Editorial magazine pages: drop-cap "C" body in serif, red serif statements with highlighted phrases.
- **Product-as-chart**: stacked red gift boxes become a bar chart; thin red line charts.
- Crop-mark corners; chapter tabs along the bottom; Lunar-New-Year ink-wash variant.
- *Steal*: paper + cut-out + serif editorial, product-as-data, crop marks.

### Chanel — 2024 marketing plan keynote → **maison** (white/blush variant)
- **Interlock typography**: one giant wide-sans word (STYLE, PEARLS, 1921, BIGGER, UNIVERSE, EFFICACY) with the bottle/hand/plane passing *through* the letters.
- Pale blush `#EDE3E6` / pure white, black type, almost nothing else on the slide.
- Product grids (pattern of bottles), "#1" with products, lipstick replacing the "0" in "2025".
- Ultra-wide LED strips (≈ 3:1) for the stage.
- *Steal*: interlock word, product-replaces-a-letter, radical restraint.

### Lacoste — annual keynote (2025) → **editorial** with brand device
- **Brand tape frame**: thin white strips carrying micro brand text divide the slide into panels (court-line feeling).
- Celebrity/campaign photography in triptychs; name labels bottom ("WANG YIBO").
- "MULTI-**CATEGORY** BRAND" — light + bold in one line, bold word in brand color.
- *Steal*: a frame device derived from the brand's world (court lines → tape).

### Puma — annual keynote (2025) → **sport**
- Condensed bold caps, **outline echo of the same word behind the solid one**, circular rotating "SS2025" text badges.
- Neon yellow on black, map silhouette in olive; LED-wall panoramas.
- *Steal*: outline echo, circular text badge, one neon accent.

### Tencent — business PPT & template (2025) → **corporate**
- **Logo-derived pixel mosaic** (blue squares) overlapping photo edges; diagonal white wedge cutting photos.
- Pale blue gradient base, white rounded cards, **one card filled in brand blue**; italic big stats (15 % / 20 % / 57 %).
- Breadcrumb chips at the top ("Agenda › 01 02 03 04"); template pages: icon library, chart pages, agenda.
- *Steal*: motif from the logo, highlighted-card pattern, template documentation pages.

### Regnology — RegTech Convention keynote (2025) → **stage** (teal variant)
- Deep teal/navy gradients, glowing light streaks, dot-matrix fields, corner brackets.
- **One phrase per slide inside a glassy capsule** ("Future-ready", "Efficiency"); neon-green logo as the only accent.
- *Steal*: glass capsule + single phrase for big-room pacing.

### Toyota — Kenshiki Forum keynote (2024) → **stage**
- Hero car photography, title bold first line + light lines, **thin red vertical bar** before speaker name/title.
- Specs right-aligned with a vertical rule; LED panoramas for the stage set.
- *Steal*: brand-red tick bar as the only accent; speaker lower-thirds.

### Ledger — keynote & template (2023) → **grid**
- Visible **module grid** with 1px lines, monospace caps, lime `#D4F06B` + black + light grey.
- `[LEDGER]` bracket logo frame reused as a label device; top meta bar "10.12.2021 /// LA GAÎTÉ LYRIQUE".
- Line-art isometric icons, glowing lime on black, bento layouts, device mockups.
- *Steal*: modules + brackets + meta bar for any tech/data deck.

### Dom Pérignon — brand presentations (2024) → **stage** (black/green)
- Black stage, prism light flares, glowing green bottle/shield.
- **Outline condensed line + solid line** ("THE BEGINNING / OF A NEW CHAPTER"); tiny wide-tracked agenda.
- Triptych columns ELEVATION / ASPIRATION / EMOTION; a timeline of limited editions in dark tiles.
- *Steal*: outline+solid title, darkness as luxury, triptych keywords.

## Cross-case patterns (frequency across the 25 cases)
- Mixed-weight or two-color title: ~80 % of cases.
- Persistent chrome/footer bar with micro caps: ~75 %.
- Full-bleed photo with text in negative space: ~90 %.
- Giant numeral / ghost word / outline echo: ~60 % (all Tier-1 luxury & keynote work).
- One highlighted card/bar among muted ones: ~55 %.
- Pills / glass capsules / ring arrows: ~50 % (all 2023–2025 work).
- Product or person layered through type or breaking a frame: ~45 %.
